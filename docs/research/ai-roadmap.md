# FoodLens AI Layer — Product Roadmap

## Vision

Transform FoodLens from a reactive scanning app into a proactive food intelligence platform. Instead of the user going to the supermarket and scanning products one by one, the AI goes out and hunts for products that match their requirements.

## Phase 1: Smart Alternatives (Build Now)

When a user views a product that scores poorly or contains their avoided allergens, the AI immediately researches and presents the best alternatives available at Woolworths — factoring in nutrition scores, additive safety, allergen compatibility, price, and size.

## Phase 2: AI Product Hunter

Users describe what they want ("healthy muesli bars, no added sugar, under $1 per bar"). The AI agent systematically searches Woolworths, analyzes every matching product against the user's criteria, and delivers a ranked evaluation. Runs on a schedule — weekly alerts for new products matching saved searches.

## Phase 3: SMS/Text Interface

"Text your nutritionist" — users text a product name or photo and get instant analysis back. Built on Twilio. Removes all friction from the experience.

## Phase 4: Personalized Email Digest

Weekly email: new products matching profile, price drops on favourites, reformulation warnings, category deep dives, community highlights.

## Phase 5: Budget-Aware Shopping Lists

Given a weekly grocery budget and family dietary needs, the AI constructs an optimized shopping list that maximizes nutritional quality within the budget. Flags half-price specials on qualifying products.

## Phase 6: Deep Research Reports

"Do a deep dive on protein powders available in Australia." The AI surveys every product, analyzes ingredients, calculates cost-per-gram, cross-references evidence, and delivers a comprehensive report.

## Phase 7: B2B Intelligence

Aggregate anonymized consumer demand signals into product development briefs for manufacturers and retailers. The consumer scanning data IS the product.

## Phase 8: MCP Server + API

Publish FoodLens as infrastructure — an MCP server that any AI assistant can query for Australian food data, ingredient safety, and personalized recommendations.
